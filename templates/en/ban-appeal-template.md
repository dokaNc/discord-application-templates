# Discord Ban Appeal Template: Questions, Examples & Replies

A Discord ban appeal template to copy: 5 core questions, 15 more to pick from, reply messages for accepted and declined appeals, and how to review them fairly.

Full guide, with tips and examples: https://toscio.com/guides/discord-ban-appeal-template/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

## The 5-question ban appeal template

1. Why were you banned?
2. Why should we lift your ban?
3. What will change?
4. When were you banned?
5. Anything else?

## More ban appeal questions to pick from

1. What is your Discord username?
2. Which moderator banned you, if you know?
3. What was the ban reason you were given?
4. Do you think the ban was fair? Why or why not?
5. Have you been warned, muted or banned on this server before?
6. Which rule do you think you broke?
7. If you were a moderator, how would you have handled what you did?
8. Is there anything you want to say to the people involved?
9. Why do you want to come back to this server?
10. Have you used another account to join the server since your ban?
11. What will you do differently if the same situation happens again?
12. Are you willing to start back with limited permissions?
13. Can you share any context we might have missed, such as screenshots?
14. Have you read the server rules again since your ban?
15. How can we contact you about this appeal?
