# Member application

> Join the community

To access the server, introduce yourself by clicking Apply. A team member will review your request as soon as possible.

Ready-to-use form from Toscio's templates (/toscio setup). Use case: https://toscio.com/use-cases/member-applications/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

1. **Your first name or nickname** _(short text)_
2. **Your age** _(short text)_
3. **Introduce yourself in a few lines** _(long text)_
4. **Why do you want to join?** _(long text)_
5. **Have you read and accepted the rules?** _(short text)_
6. **How did you find us?** _(short text, optional)_
7. **Your interests** _(short text, optional)_
