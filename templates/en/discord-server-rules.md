# Discord Server Rules Template: Rules to Copy & Paste

Copy and paste Discord server rules: a short and a full rules template, moderator and voice chat rules, a free rules generator and a rules channel setup.

Full guide, with tips and examples: https://toscio.com/guides/discord-server-rules-template/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

## Copy and paste Discord rules: the short version

1. Be respectful. No insults, harassment or personal attacks.
2. No hate speech, slurs or discrimination of any kind.
3. No spam: no message floods, walls of emojis or excessive pings.
4. No advertising or self-promotion without staff permission, including in DMs.
5. Keep content in the right channels and stay on topic.
6. No NSFW, gore or shocking content outside age-restricted channels.
7. Do not share anyone's personal information, including your own.
8. No impersonating members, staff or bots.
9. Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
10. Listen to the staff. If you disagree with a decision, message them privately.

## Full Discord server rules template

1. Be respectful. Disagreeing is fine, attacking the person is not.
2. No hate speech, slurs or jokes targeting race, religion, gender, sexuality or disability.
3. No harassment, threats or bullying, in the server or in DMs with other members.
4. No spam: message floods, copypasta, excessive caps, emojis or mentions.
5. Don't ping staff or roles without a good reason.
6. No advertising: servers, social media, products or referral links, unless staff allow it.
7. Use each channel for its purpose. Read the channel topic before posting.
8. Use English in public channels, unless a channel says otherwise.
9. No NSFW, gore or shocking content, including in names, avatars and status.
10. No illegal content, piracy, cheats or account selling.
11. No scams, phishing links or fake giveaways. Report them to the staff.
12. Don't share personal information: real names, addresses, photos or accounts, yours or anyone else's.
13. No doxxing, raiding or organizing attacks against other servers.
14. No impersonating members, staff, bots or public figures.
15. Keep usernames and nicknames readable and appropriate.
16. No alt accounts to dodge a punishment. Ban evasion leads to a permanent ban.
17. Don't argue about a moderation decision in public. Contact the staff privately.
18. Follow Discord's Terms of Service (discord.com/terms) and Community Guidelines (discord.com/guidelines).
19. Staff have the final say, including on situations these rules don't list.
20. Rules can change. It is your job to check them from time to time.

## Voice chat rules

1. Respect whoever is talking. Don't talk over people or interrupt on purpose.
2. No earrape: loud noises, soundboards or music at high volume.
3. Use push-to-talk if your microphone picks up background noise.
4. No voice changers or soundboards unless the channel allows it.
5. Don't record or stream others without their consent.
6. Don't hop between channels to disrupt conversations.
7. Music bots stay in the music channels.
8. The same rules apply in voice as in text: no hate speech, harassment or NSFW.

## Discord moderator rules for your staff

1. Stay calm and polite, even when a member is not.
2. Use the lightest action that solves the problem: a warning before a timeout, a timeout before a ban.
3. Log every warning, timeout, kick and ban in the staff log, with the reason.
4. Never punish someone for disagreeing with you, only for breaking a rule.
5. Treat friends, popular members and other staff like everyone else.
6. Don't moderate a conflict you are part of. Ask another staff member.
7. Handle arguments about decisions in private, never in public channels.
8. Escalate to an admin when you are unsure, or before any permanent ban.
9. Don't use staff permissions for anything other than moderation.
10. Never share staff discussions, logs or reports outside the staff team.
11. Tell the team in advance when you need a break.
12. Admit mistakes. If you punished the wrong person, fix it and apologize.

## Roleplay and FiveM server rules

1. No RDM (random deathmatch): don't attack or kill another player without a roleplay reason.
2. No VDM (vehicle deathmatch): don't use a vehicle as a weapon without a roleplay reason.
3. NLR (new life rule): after your character is downed and respawns, forget the events that led to it.
4. No metagaming: don't use information your character couldn't know, such as a stream or Discord.
5. No powergaming: don't force actions on others or do things that are impossible in real life.
6. Value your life (Fear RP): act as if your character's life matters when threatened.
7. No combat logging: don't disconnect or leave to avoid a roleplay situation.
8. Stay in character in game. Keep out-of-character talk to the OOC channels.
9. No fail RP: don't break immersion with behavior your character wouldn't have.
10. No exploiting bugs, mods or cheats. Report bugs to the staff.
11. Respect the safe zones (hospital, police station, spawn) set by the server.
12. Report rule breaks with a clip after the scene, not in the middle of it.
