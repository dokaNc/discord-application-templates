# FiveM Whitelist Application Questions: 53 for GTA RP Servers

53 FiveM whitelist application questions for GTA RP servers: backstory, RP rules like RDM and NLR, scenarios, plus red flags and a ready 5-question template.

Full guide, with tips and examples: https://toscio.com/guides/fivem-whitelist-application-questions/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

## Out-of-character basics

1. How old are you (out of character)?
2. What is your FiveM or Cfx.re account name?
3. What country and time zone are you in?
4. Do you have a working microphone?
5. Have you read the full server rules?
6. How did you find our server?
7. Have you ever been banned from a FiveM server? If yes, why?
8. Do you stream or record your RP? Where?

## RP experience

1. How long have you been roleplaying?
2. Which RP servers have you played on, and for how long?
3. What kind of RP do you enjoy most: civilian, criminal, police, EMS or business?
4. What is your favorite RP moment so far, and why?
5. In your opinion, what makes a good roleplay scene?
6. Why do you want to play on our server rather than another one?
7. What does serious RP mean to you?
8. Have you ever played a whitelisted job like police, EMS or a gang?

## Character and backstory

1. What is your character's full name and age?
2. Where was your character born and raised?
3. Why did your character come to Los Santos?
4. Write your character's backstory in at least 150 words.
5. What are your character's main personality traits?
6. What are your character's flaws and fears?
7. What are your character's short-term and long-term goals?
8. What job would your character look for first?
9. What would your character never do, even for money?
10. Who are the most important people in your character's life?
11. How do you see your character evolving over the next few months?

## Rules and RP terms

1. What does RDM mean? Give an example.
2. What does VDM mean?
3. Explain the New Life Rule (NLR) in your own words.
4. What is metagaming? Give an example.
5. What is powergaming?
6. What does fail RP mean?
7. What is fear RP, also called value of life?
8. What is combat logging, and why is it forbidden?
9. What is the difference between OOC and IC?
10. When is it acceptable to go out of character during a scene?
11. What is a character kill (CK), and who decides one on our server?

## In-character scenarios

1. Two armed people hold your character at gunpoint. What does your character do?
2. During a scene, another player kills you with no RP reason. What do you do?
3. A friend tells you on Discord where a rival gang is hiding. Can your character use that information?
4. Your game crashes in the middle of a police chase. What do you do when you reconnect?
5. Your character is downed and respawns at the hospital. What do they remember, and where can they go?
6. A police officer pulls your character over for speeding. Write the first few lines of the scene.
7. A player is shouting out-of-character insults in proximity voice. How do you react?
8. Your character walks into an ongoing store robbery as a civilian. What do they do?
9. A new player is lost and asks out-of-character questions in game. How do you help without breaking immersion?
10. You lose a long scene you invested a lot of time in. How do you react?

## Server-specific questions

1. Which whitelisted job are you interested in, if any?
2. Do you plan to start with legal or illegal RP?
3. Do you already know players on our server?
4. How many hours per week do you expect to play?
5. Is there anything else our staff should know about you?

## The 5-question short version

1. How old are you (out of character)?
2. What is your character's full name?
3. What is your RP experience?
4. What is your character's backstory?
5. What do RDM and NLR mean?
