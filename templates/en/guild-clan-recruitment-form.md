# Guild recruitment

> Join the guild

The guild is recruiting! Click Apply with your character and availability, an officer will answer by direct message.

Ready-to-use form from Toscio's templates (/toscio setup). Use case: https://toscio.com/use-cases/guild-recruitment/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

1. **Your in-game character** — Name and server (or world). _(short text)_
2. **Your class or role** _(short text)_
3. **Your level or gear score** _(short text)_
4. **Your experience** — Past guilds, content cleared (raids, dungeons, PvP). _(long text)_
5. **When are you available?** _(short text)_
6. **Why our guild?** _(long text, optional)_
7. **Link to your logs or profile** — Optional: raid logs, armory, profile page… _(short text, optional)_
