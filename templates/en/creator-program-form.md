# Creator program

> Join the creator program

Creating content about our game or community? Click Apply to join the creator program.

Ready-to-use form from Toscio's templates (/toscio setup). Use case: https://toscio.com/use-cases/creator-program/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

1. **Your creator name** _(short text)_
2. **Link to your main channel** — Twitch, YouTube, TikTok… the direct link to your channel. _(short text)_
3. **Followers or average viewers** _(short text)_
4. **How often do you post?** _(short text)_
5. **What content would you make with us?** _(long text)_
6. **Your other social links** _(short text, optional)_
7. **Why this program?** _(long text, optional)_
