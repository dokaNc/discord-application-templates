# Minecraft Whitelist

> Join the Minecraft server

The server is whitelisted. Click Apply and staff will add you once your application is accepted.

Ready-to-use form from Toscio's templates (/toscio setup). Use case: https://toscio.com/use-cases/minecraft-whitelist/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

1. **Your Minecraft username** — Exactly as in game, capital letters included. _(short text)_
2. **Java or Bedrock?** _(short text)_
3. **Your age** _(short text)_
4. **Why do you want to join?** _(long text)_
5. **What will you do on the server?** — Building, redstone, trading, exploring… _(long text)_
6. **Servers you played on before** _(short text, optional)_
7. **How did you find the server?** _(short text, optional)_
