# Partnership request

> Become a partner

Running a server or a project and want to partner with us? Click Apply: the team reviews every request.

Ready-to-use form from Toscio's templates (/toscio setup). Use case: https://toscio.com/use-cases/partnership-requests/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

1. **Your server or project name** _(short text)_
2. **Invite link or website** — A permanent link to your Discord server or your website. _(short text)_
3. **Member count** _(short text)_
4. **Describe your server** — Theme, audience, activity. _(long text)_
5. **What do you offer?** — Ad exchange, joint event, partner channel… _(long text)_
6. **Your role on that server** _(short text, optional)_
7. **Anything else?** _(long text, optional)_
