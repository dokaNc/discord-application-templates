# RP Whitelist

> Join the whitelist

Want to join the server? Click Apply and introduce your character. Staff will answer by direct message.

Ready-to-use form from Toscio's templates (/toscio setup). Use case: https://toscio.com/use-cases/fivem-whitelist/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

1. **Your age (out of character)** _(short text)_
2. **Your character's full name** _(short text)_
3. **Your RP experience** — Servers you played on, for how long, your play style. _(long text)_
4. **Your character's backstory** — Their past and their goals in the city. _(long text)_
5. **What do RDM and NLR mean?** — Show us you know the basic rules. _(long text)_
6. **When are you available?** _(short text, optional)_
7. **How did you find the server?** _(short text, optional)_
