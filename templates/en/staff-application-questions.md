# Discord Staff & Mod Application Questions: 58 to Copy & Paste

58 Discord staff and mod application questions to copy: moderator scenarios, what good answers look like, red flags and a 5-question template to use today.

Full guide, with tips and examples: https://toscio.com/guides/discord-staff-application-questions/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

## About the applicant

1. How old are you?
2. What is your time zone?
3. How long have you been a member of this server?
4. Which channels are you most active in?
5. What languages do you speak fluently?
6. Tell us a bit about yourself outside of Discord.
7. How would your friends describe you in three words?
8. What name do you prefer staff and members to use for you?

## Moderation experience

1. Have you been staff on a Discord server before? Which ones, and what was your role?
2. Roughly how many members did those servers have?
3. Why did you leave your previous staff positions?
4. Which moderation bots or tools have you used?
5. What is the difference between a timeout, a kick and a ban, and when would you use each?
6. Have you ever configured AutoMod rules or word filters?
7. What was the hardest situation you handled as a moderator, and what did you learn from it?
8. Have you ever been warned, muted or banned on a server? What happened?
9. Have you handled support tickets or member reports before?

## Motivation and fit

1. Why do you want to join our staff team?
2. Why should we choose you over other applicants?
3. In your own words, what is a moderator's job?
4. What is one thing you like about how this server is run, and one thing you would improve?
5. How do you react when someone criticizes a decision you made?
6. What does a healthy community look like to you?
7. What would you do in your first week as a staff member?

## Moderator application questions: scenarios

1. Two members are arguing in public chat and it is getting personal. What do you do, step by step?
2. A member spams the same message across several channels. How do you handle it?
3. A close friend of yours breaks a server rule. What do you do?
4. A member reports harassment in DMs but has no screenshots. How do you proceed?
5. You notice another staff member abusing their permissions. What do you do?
6. Someone posts shocking or NSFW content in a public channel and no admin is online. What are your first actions?
7. A member mentions in chat that they are under 13. How do you react?
8. A raid starts: dozens of new accounts join and spam at once. What are your first three actions?
9. A member disagrees with your warning and starts insulting you publicly. How do you respond?
10. A user posts a link promising free Nitro. What do you do?
11. A member says something that suggests they might hurt themselves. How do you react?
12. Two staff members gave a member contradictory answers. How do you fix it?
13. A popular member or content creator breaks a rule. Do you handle it differently?
14. You realize you punished the wrong person. What do you do?
15. A member keeps pushing the limits of a rule without clearly breaking it. How do you handle it?

## Rules and server knowledge

1. Which of our rules do you think is broken most often, and why?
2. Pick one of our rules and explain why it exists.
3. What would you do in a situation the rules do not cover?
4. Name two things Discord's Community Guidelines forbid that every moderator should know.
5. When should a moderator escalate to an admin instead of acting alone?
6. How would you write up a punishment so other staff can follow up on it?
7. What does “punish the behavior, not the person” mean to you?

## Availability and commitment

1. How many hours per week can you give to the staff team?
2. Which days and times are you usually online?
3. Can you join a staff meeting in voice from time to time?
4. Do you have exams, trips or work coming up that will reduce your activity?
5. Are you currently staff on other servers? How many?
6. Will you let us know in advance if you need a break?

## Helper and support roles

1. A new member asks a question already answered in the FAQ channel. How do you reply?
2. How would you welcome a new member who seems lost?
3. Pitch an event idea for this server in two or three sentences.
4. Are you comfortable speaking in voice channels?
5. How would you explain a technical problem to someone who is not technical?
6. Do you have skills that could help beyond moderation, like design, writing, bots or video?

## Staff application template: the 5-question version

1. How old are you?
2. When are you available each week?
3. What is your moderation experience?
4. Why do you want to join the staff?
5. Two members fight in chat: what do you do?
