# How to welcome new members on Discord: built-in tools, welcome bots and member approval

Set up a Discord welcome bot step by step: Discord's built-in welcome message, Onboarding, five welcome bots compared and welcome message templates to copy.

Full guide, with tips and examples: https://toscio.com/guides/discord-welcome-bot/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=templates

## General community

1. Welcome to {server}, {user}! 👋
2. Start by reading #rules, it takes one minute.
3. Pick your roles in #roles to see the channels that interest you.
4. Say hi in #general and tell us how you found us.
5. Questions? Ask in #help, someone will answer.

## Study group or class

1. Welcome to {server}, {user}! 📚
2. Read #rules and the pinned message in #start-here first.
3. Pick your subjects or your class in #roles.
4. Study sessions are announced in #events: join any of them.
5. Stuck on something? Post your question in #help with what you already tried.

## Creator community

1. Hey {user}, welcome to {server}! 🎬
2. New content is posted in #announcements: turn on notifications so you don't miss it.
3. Share your own work in #showcase and give feedback to others.
4. Please read #rules before posting links.
5. Want to collaborate? Introduce yourself in #introductions.

## Esports team or competitive community

1. Welcome to {server}, {user}! 🏆
2. Read #rules and check #schedule for practices and matches.
3. Set your game and rank in #roles to find people at your level.
4. Looking for a team? Post in #looking-for-team.
5. Tryouts are announced in #announcements.

## Gaming community

1. {user} just joined {server}! 🎮
2. Read #rules, then grab your game roles in #roles.
3. Find people to play with in #lfg.
4. Server events and game nights are in #events.
5. Report a problem or a player in #support.

## Support or help community

1. Welcome to {server}, {user}! 💬
2. Before asking, search #faq: most answers are already there.
3. Post your question in #help with as much detail as you can.
4. Be patient: everyone here helps on their free time.
5. Please read #rules, especially the part about pinging.
